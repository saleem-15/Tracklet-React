## Priority Issues (Remaining / Follow-up)

### [P2] Minor gray-on-color contrast warnings in secondary components
**What**: 14 remaining `gray-on-color` warnings reported by detector in `ActivePipelineBoard`, `AddApplicationModal`, `ApplicationDetailPanel`, and `SettingsView`.
**Why it matters**: Some text elements use `text-slate-500` or `text-slate-400` over soft color tints like `bg-blue-50` or `bg-amber-50`.
**Fix**: Use shade-matched colors (e.g., `text-blue-700` over `bg-blue-50`) for higher contrast and crispness.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] Font uniqueness / Typography character
**What**: Plus Jakarta Sans is a safe, high-quality font but commonly used across modern SaaS apps.
**Why it matters**: A slightly more distinct header font could give Tracklet a stronger visual identity.
**Fix**: Introduce a distinctive display face for headings or key metric numbers.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`
