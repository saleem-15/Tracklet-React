# Startup Context

## Company
- **Name:** Tracklet
- **One-liner:** A fast, clean, and intuitive job application workspace that keeps job seekers organized without clutter or complexity.
- **Stage:** Prototype / MVP
- **Founded:** 2025 / 2026
- **Location:** Remote
- **Legal entity:** TBD (Pre-incorporation / Sole Proprietorship)

## Product
- **What it does:** Tracklet provides a clutter-free board and table interface to track job applications across multi-stage interview pipelines. It features a built-in networking CRM (Contacts & Mentorship Hub), rich text interview preparation notes, activity histories, and fast cloud sync backed by Firebase.
- **Category:** Productivity / Career Tech
- **Platform:** Web App (Vite, Responsive Desktop & Mobile Web) + Chrome Extension
- **Tech stack:** React 19, TypeScript, TailwindCSS v4, Firebase (Firestore, Auth), Sentry, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub
- **Current state:** Prototype / MVP (in active daily use as the founder's daily driver; guest mode removed in favor of authenticated cloud sync; data privacy guaranteed with zero data selling and full user import/export, not local-first)

## Market & Empirical Validation
- **Target customer:** High-intent, relationship-driven job seekers, senior ICs/managers, and independent career coaches who prioritize targeted applications and networking over low-conversion spam bots.
- **ICP:** Employed mid-to-senior tech & white-collar professionals ($80k–$250k target salary) managing multi-stage interview loops across 15–40 high-value opportunities, requiring deep interview prep notes, contact networking history (referrals/recruiters), and webmail communication tracking.
- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM: $4.5B (Global Career Tech & Resume Services); SAM: $680M (Job Search Productivity & Tracking Tools); SOM: $4.2M (Quality-first, privacy-focused candidates & career coaching cohorts).
- **Competitors:**
  - **Tsenta (YC S26):** AI auto-apply agent monitoring 50k+ career pages, mass application submission ($19–$99/mo). Vulnerable to knockout errors, recruiter backlash, and ATS bot blacklists.
  - **Simplify (YC S21):** Free candidate extension for 1-click autofill with 3M+ users; monetizes candidate database via B2B employer sourcing fees.
  - **TealHQ:** AI resume match scoring & Kanban tracking ($29/mo or $9/wk). Bloated UI, predatory weekly billing, no autofill/networking CRM.
  - **Huntr:** Established Kanban tracker with bootcamp/university portals ($40/mo B2C). Stagnant consumer innovation, high monthly price.
  - **Wonsulting AI:** Influencer-driven AI bundle ($19.99/mo) with shallow GPT wrappers and template cold messages.
  - **Notion & Spreadsheets:** The ~70% status-quo default. Free, customizable, but completely manual with zero automation or email intelligence.
- **Positioning:** The distraction-free, privacy-first career command center for serious candidates. "Anti-bot, high-touch, zero clutter."
- **Empirical Validation Ground Truth (TAP Cohort):**
  - Reached out to 10 active job-seeking peers via 1-on-1 Slack DMs.
  - Results: 4 signups (40%), 2 excited (20%), 1 daily driver (10%).
  - **Monetization Reality:** Jobless cohort students are excellent design partners and feedback providers, but have **zero willingness/budget to pay**. Revenue must come from employed career transitioners / senior ICs or institutional coaching/bootcamp software licenses, NOT student cohorts.

## Business Model
- **Revenue model:** Non-predatory consumer freemium + 6-month "Search Pass" or low-friction monthly subscription; future "Tracklet for Coaches" cohort license.
- **Pricing:** Free Core (up to 20 active applications + Extension clipper + 10 contacts); $39 "Job Search Pass" (6 months active + lifetime read-only); $9/month flat.
- **Current MRR/ARR:** $0 (Pre-revenue)
